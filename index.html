How to Turn Data Into Clear Insight

01

Clear Question

What is our question in plain English?

02

Explore the Data

Investigate each column individually. Build graphics and simple summaries to get a clearer picture of what you are working with.

03

Model Selection

Find the model that fits the question and the data assumptions.

04

Detail Findings

Make note of what you learned, what worked, and what didn't.

05

Next Steps

What other questions arise? What should we investigate further? Repeat the process.
