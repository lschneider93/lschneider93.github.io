Cancer and genetic testing researchMyriad Genetics · 2022 – 2025

Can a clinic find more patients who qualify for hereditary cancer testing, and get them tested?

Question
Clinical and lab teams needed study designs, sample sizes, and analyses they could defend, and data they could trust before modeling.
What I did
Statistical modeling (survival, logistic, mixed-effects), power and sample-size planning, reproducible R workflows with data-quality checks, and review of other statisticians' analyses.
What it showed
Co-authored a peer-reviewed study in Obstetrics and Gynecology (2025) where online screening and virtual education roughly doubled the share of eligible patients who completed genetic testing.
Why it matters
More patients at hereditary risk get identified and tested, and researchers get answers they can stand behind.

Pickleball analytics · computer visionJan 2026 – Current

What can a match video reveal about a player's decisions, and how can it help them improve?

Question
Players record games but have no simple way to review their shot choices. Can one camera's footage turn into data they can learn from?
What I did
Built a computer vision pipeline that finds the ball, players, and court in every frame, marks each hit and bounce, places them on a court map, and names serves and returns. Every model is scored against answers I label by hand.
What it shows
Runs end to end on a single rally from a fixed camera. Shot naming and player identity across rallies are not yet reliable, and one camera cannot measure ball height.
Why it matters
Coaching feedback today depends on a coach being there. A measured record of a session makes review possible for anyone.

Working on it: splitting whole sessions into games, naming every shot type, and checking player identity against hand labels.

rsnodas · M.S. thesisUtah State University · 2021 – 2022

Can combining two data sources improve estimates of the water stored in Utah's snow?

Question
Snow stations measure snow water accurately but only at about a hundred points. National grids cover everything but struggle in mountains, which is where the water is.
What I did
Wrote and published rsnodas, an R package that downloads both sources, fits a local model to the stations, and blends it with the national grid, letting station density decide the weighting.
What it showed
The blend was more stable than either input and edged out the national grid in most years, but the gain was marginal. The local model was the weak link, and I said so.
Why it matters
Water managers get a reproducible way to combine sources, and an honest read of how much it helps. The thesis is published.
