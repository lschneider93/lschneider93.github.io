My Approach

How I work through a question, on every project.

01

Clear Question

I start by putting the question in plain English.

02

Explore the Data

I investigate each column, build graphics and simple summaries, and get a clear picture of what I'm working with.

03

Model Selection

I find the model that fits the question and the data assumptions.

04

Validate and Explain

I check that results hold up, then write down what I learned, what worked, and what didn't.

05

Next Steps

I note the new questions that came up, decide what to investigate next, and repeat the process.
