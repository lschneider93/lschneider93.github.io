Snow Water Equivalent Mapping in Utah

Built rsnodas, an open-source R package that downloads national gridded data (SNODAS, PRISM), blends it with SNOTEL station observations, and maps snow water equivalent across Utah with an ensemble of GAMs.

The ensemble showed marginal error improvements. Honest results that point future research in the right direction.

Approach

How I Turn Data Into Decisions

01

Define the Decision

What action will this analysis support? What would success look like, and which errors would be most costly?

02

Audit the Evidence

Understand how the data was collected, identify missingness and bias, verify definitions, and challenge assumptions before modeling.

03

Build & Validate

Start with an interpretable baseline, compare appropriate alternatives, and evaluate against the metric that matters to the decision.

04

Communicate the Limits

Translate findings into clear recommendations while documenting uncertainty, trade-offs, and where the result should not be trusted.

AI-accelerated. Statistically validated.

I use AI development tools to move faster, but generated output doesn't decide what ships. I verify systems against hand-labeled ground truth, measure precision and recall, test calibration, and document failure modes before recommending action.
