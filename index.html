Pickleball Analytics — Computer Vision Rally Tracking

January 2026 – Present · Active Development

An eight-stage Python pipeline that turns one camera angle of game footage into an annotated rally video and a structured stat sheet: Kalman-filtered ball tracking, player keypoints, court homography, bounce and hit events, and shot classification.

Every accuracy claim is evaluated against hand-labeled ground truth and tracked release-over-release — and when measurement uncertainty exceeds the ball's radius, the system reports an honest amber CLOSE instead of a confident wrong call.

Snow Water Equivalent Mapping in Utah

Built rsnodas, an open-source R package that blends national gridded data (SNODAS, PRISM) with SNOTEL station observations to map snow water equivalent with an ensemble of GAMs. The ensemble showed marginal error improvements — honest results that point future research in the right direction.

Approach

How I Turn Data Into Decisions

01

Define the Decision

What action will this analysis support? What would success look like — and which errors would be most costly to make?

02

Audit the Evidence

Understand how the data was collected, identify missingness and bias, verify definitions, and challenge assumptions before modeling.

03

Build & Validate

Start with an interpretable baseline, compare appropriate alternatives, and evaluate against the metric that matters to the decision.

04

Communicate the Limits

Translate findings into clear recommendations — while documenting uncertainty, trade-offs, and where the result should not be trusted.

AI-accelerated. Statistically validated.

I use AI development tools to move faster — but generated output doesn't decide what ships. I verify systems against hand-labeled ground truth, measure precision and recall, test calibration, and document failure modes before recommending action.
