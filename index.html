Cancer and genetic testing researchMyriad Genetics · 2022 – 2025

Can a clinic find more patients who qualify for hereditary cancer testing, and get them tested?

Question
Research and lab teams needed studies that could answer their question, and data they could trust before modeling.
What I did
Planned sample sizes, built reproducible R workflows with data-quality checks, fit survival, logistic, and mixed-effects models, and reviewed other statisticians' analyses.
What it showed
Co-authored a 2025 study in Obstetrics and Gynecology where online screening and virtual education roughly doubled the share of eligible patients who completed genetic testing.
Why it matters
More patients at hereditary risk get found and tested.

Pickleball analyticsJan 2026 – Current

What can a match video reveal about a player's decisions, and how can it help them improve?

Question
Players record games but have no simple way to review their shot choices. Can one camera's footage become data they can learn from?
What I did
Built a computer vision pipeline that finds the ball, players, and court, marks each hit and bounce, places them on a court map, and names serves and returns. Every model is scored against hand-labeled answers.
What it shows
Works end to end on a single rally from a fixed camera. Full shot naming and player identity across rallies are not yet reliable.
Why it matters
A measured record of a session makes review possible without a coach present.

Working on it: splitting sessions into games, naming every shot type, checking player identity against hand labels.

Estimating the water stored in Utah's snowpackM.S. thesis · Utah State · 2021 – 2022

Can combining two data sources improve estimates of the water stored in snow?

Question
Snow stations are accurate but sparse. National grids cover everything but struggle in mountains, where the water is.
What I did
Wrote and published rsnodas, an R package that downloads both sources, fits a local model to the stations, and blends it with the national grid, weighted by station density.
What it showed
The blend was more stable than either input but improved on the national grid only slightly. The local model was the weak link.
Why it matters
A reproducible way to combine the sources water managers already have, with an honest measure of the gain.

R · Spatial modeling · Model comparison · Published package
